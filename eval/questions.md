# MudraMitra - Evaluation Questions

These questions evaluate the MudraMitra RAG chatbot using MUDRA scheme records.

## Questions

1. What are the different categories available under the Pradhan Mantri Mudra Yojana (PMMY)?
2. Which Mudra category is suitable for a loan amount of Rs. 3 lakh?
3. What is the maximum loan amount available under the Shishu category?
4. What is the loan amount range under the Kishor category?
5. What is the loan amount range under the Tarun category?
6. What is the exact current interest rate offered by SBI for a Rs. 3 lakh Mudra loan?
7. What is the maximum loan amount available under the Tarun Plus category?
8. What is the eligibility condition for applying under the Tarun Plus category?
9. What types of businesses are represented in the MudraMitra records?
10. According to the records, who decides the repayment period and terms?

## Unanswerable Question

Question 6 is intentionally unanswerable from the current knowledge base.

Expected chatbot behavior: The chatbot should explain that the exact current SBI interest rate is not available in its records. It should not guess or invent a rate.

## Evaluation Criteria

1. Does the chatbot understand the question?
2. Does it retrieve relevant MUDRA records?
3. Does it provide accurate answers based on the retrieved information?
4. Does it respond in simple language?
5. Does it clearly state when information is unavailable?

Goal: The chatbot should provide reliable answers using MUDRA records and avoid hallucinating information.
